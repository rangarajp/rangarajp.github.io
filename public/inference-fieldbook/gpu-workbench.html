The line uses measured proxies (237.5 GB/s copy and 43.73 TFLOP/s GEMM). It is an illustrative empirical roofline, not a validated device peak. Points use idealized traffic assumptions.

Why it matters: The rising side means workers wait for data; the flat side means computation is the stronger constraint. The bend tells us where an optimization strategy may need to change.
