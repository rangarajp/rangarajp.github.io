# 3.1 — KV cache deep dive

Notebook: [`3.1_kv_cache.ipynb`](./3.1_kv_cache.ipynb)

## Goal

Understand KV caching end-to-end: metrics with vs without, how tensors live on GPU, how vLLM pages them, and advanced reuse (prefix caching).

## Map

| § | What you do | Takeaway |
|---|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Mental model | K/V are fixed per token → safe to store |
| 2 | Full forward vs cached decode vs `L` | ratio grows with context (more visible on 7B than tiny models) |
| 3 | Bytes formula | HBM grows with tokens × layers × kv_heads |
| 4 | Inspect `past_key_values` | prefill writes; decode grows seq dim |
| 5 | vLLM engine knobs | paged block pool, not contiguous slabs |
| 6 | HF prefix reuse | MISS full prompt vs HIT reused system KV |
| 7 | Checklist | table of techniques |

## Key formulas

```text
bytes/token ≈ 2 × L × n_kv_heads × head_dim × sizeof(dtype)
```

Decode is often **HBM-bandwidth bound** (read past KV every step). Prefill is often **compute bound**.
